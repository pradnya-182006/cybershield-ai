6-Label Multi-Label Detection

✅

Safe

Constructive, harmless language that respects community standards.

☣️

Toxic

Rude, hostile or disrespectful language targeting individuals or groups.

💢

Insult

Demeaning or degrading terms that attack a person's character or identity.

🚨

Threat

Language implying physical harm or violence. Always escalated for human review.

🔞

Obscene

Profane or vulgar language that violates content guidelines.

😏

Sarcasm

Mocking, ironic or passive-aggressive tone — evaluated independently from toxicity.
